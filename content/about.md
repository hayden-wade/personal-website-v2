# Engineer by profession. Project starter by nature.

I’m Hayden, a Product Security Engineer based in Brisbane. My work sits where cybersecurity, electrical engineering and complex systems meet.

I studied Electrical Engineering at UNSW Canberra, then spent a decade in the Royal Australian Air Force. That took me through cyber incident-response training, capability planning and the practical work of getting Defence systems into service and keeping them supported. A Master of Project Management added another way of looking at the same problem: how to turn a good technical idea into something that actually gets delivered.

Today I work at Boeing Defence Australia, bringing that background into product security engineering.

## The workshop is the other classroom

Away from work, I tend to learn by taking on projects that are slightly bigger than they first appear. The E30 respray was a particularly good example: a hail-damaged daily driver turned into months of bodywork, rust repair, sanding and reassembly.

Cars bring engineering back into the physical world. Wiring has to fit somewhere. A connector needs to survive vibration. A repair has to work next week as well as today. I like the combination of theory, judgement and hands-on problem-solving.

That is also where [ChassisWire](/projects/chassiswire/) started: wanting a better way to understand and document the electrical systems in the cars I work on.

## Outside the working week

Old BMWs take up a fair amount of my attention, but they are not the whole picture. I enjoy photography, travelling, finding a good café and paying probably too much attention to architecture and interiors. Mid-century design is a recurring weakness.

This site is a place to collect the useful bits: the projects, the mistakes, the photographs and the things I would want to know before doing it again.
