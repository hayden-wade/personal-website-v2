# Restoring an E30

From hail-damaged daily to a full DIY respray.

A seven-part journal documenting the bodywork, rust repair, paint and reassembly of my 1990 BMW E30 318i in Glacier Blue.

The project spans the 2021 hailstorm, the preparation and respray through 2023, and reflections on the result in 2026. I prepared and reassembled the car; a painter applied the final finish. The full paint correction remains unfinished.
