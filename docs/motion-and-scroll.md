# Motion and Scroll

## Direction

Keep the agreed page structure unchanged. The Constance Souville reference applies to the *feel* of the scrolling and motion, not the information architecture.

## Motion principles

- Smooth, fluid, restrained scrolling
- Slight inertial feel without becoming floaty
- Subtle image parallax
- Headings can reveal through clipping/masks
- Elements should enter at slightly different vertical offsets rather than everything simply fading
- The hero should transition naturally into the main interface
- The left table of contents fades in after the hero and remains sticky through the main content
- Active section state should be subtle: line extension, weight shift, or small movement
- Projects and photography can use more motion than experience and writing

## Avoid

- Generic exaggerated smooth-scroll effects
- Excessive animation on every element
- Motion that compromises readability
