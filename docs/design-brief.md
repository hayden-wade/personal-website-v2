# Design Brief

## Direction

A warm, modern, technical personal portfolio with an editorial feel.

The homepage opens with a full-screen hero, then transitions into the main site as a left-hand table of contents fades in. The overall structure remains restrained and content-led, with smooth, fluid scrolling and subtle motion.

## Homepage sections

1. About
2. Experience
3. Projects
4. Writing
5. Photography
6. Contact

## Hero

- Full viewport on first load
- Large `HAYDEN WADE` typography
- `PRODUCT SECURITY ENGINEER + BUILDER`
- `CYBERSECURITY · ENGINEERING · OLD CARS`
- Scenic hero photography with the red E30 318is small in frame
- Lots of negative space

## Main content

- Sticky left table of contents after the hero
- Active section indicator
- Editorial typography and generous spacing
- Selected experience rather than a full CV on the homepage
- Dedicated project and long-form writing pages

## E30 respray series

The homepage writing item should open an E30 respray series landing page, which then presents the seven individual posts in an editorial, image-led sequence.
